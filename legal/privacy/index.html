Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

This policy explains how StoopBook (“the app”) handles information on iPhone.

Visit records on this iPhone

Rounds, stoops, access notes, and saved visit readouts stay in the app’s local store on that iPhone. They are a planning book, not a medical chart, and they are not uploaded as a patient record.

Data not linked to you

The following data may be collected but is not linked to your identity, and may be used for third-party advertising:

  • Usage Data
  • Advertising Data

How that data is used

Usage Data means product interaction with the app (for example, which kits and screens you open). Advertising Data means information used to show or measure ads. These categories are used for third-party advertising. They are not joined to your name, email, or the household nicknames in the round book, and they are not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Device permissions

StoopBook does not request access to your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, or tracking permission. Map pins are coordinates you already stored. The app never asks for a live location fix.

This website

Opening Privacy or Terms loads these pages from Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process limited technical request data, such as an IP address, to deliver the page under its own terms. That visit is not used to identify you inside the round book.

Deleting data

Settings → Delete All Data clears local visit records, blocks, and circuits, then returns the introduction. Uninstalling the app also removes the on-device store. Advertising partners may retain their own records according to their policies.

Children

StoopBook is a general-audience planning tool. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the StoopBook product page on the App Store.
